The History and Philosophy of Science Division of the Mississippi Academy of Sciences Ninety First Annual Meeting

MARCH 18 & 19, 2027  |  Biloxi, Mississippi

Call For Abstracts

KEYNOTE ADDRESS

We welcome submissions from all areas of philosophy of science and on all topics in the history of science (very broadly construed in both cases). Submissions by graduate and undergraduate students are encouraged. 

Abstracts of up to 500 words should be submitted by 11:59 pm (Central) on November 15, 2026. Select “Submission” on the initial page.

Choose “History and Philosophy of Science” as the division at the very bottom of the submission form. 

Presentation length will be 20 minutes. 

Send questions to [Greg Johnson](mailto:gregory.johnson@msstate.edu)
